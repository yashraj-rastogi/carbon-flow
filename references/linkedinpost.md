🌿 Just shipped **Carbon-Flow** for **#PromptWars Virtual — Challenge 3**.

Most sustainability apps fail not because of bad data — but because of friction and zero consequence.

You download them. You log once. Then life happens and you never open them again.

**Carbon-Flow fixes that in two ways:**

① **Zero friction.** Snap a photo of your utility bill or record a voice note. Gemini AI extracts everything — no manual entry, ever.

② **Real consequence.** Your dashboard is a living entity. Log consistently and it blooms into a lush 🌿 Oasis. Go quiet for 24 hours and it decays into an 🏭 Industrial Wasteland — glitching, smoking, alarming. The UI itself becomes the feedback loop.

Behavior change doesn't come from better charts. It comes from making inaction *visible*.
